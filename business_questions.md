\# Business Questions

The goal of this analysis is to answer the following questions.

\---

\#\# Executive Questions

1\. How much total revenue was generated?

2\. How many transactions occurred?

3\. How many units were sold?

4\. What is the average transaction value?

\---

\#\# Store Performance

5\. Which store generates the most revenue?

6\. Which store processes the most orders?

7\. Which store sells the most units?

8\. Which store has the highest average transaction value?

\---

\#\# Product Performance

9\. Which product categories generate the most revenue?

10\. Which individual products generate the most revenue?

11\. Which products underperform?

12\. Do a small number of products generate most revenue?

\---

\#\# Time Analysis

13\. Which hour generates the most revenue?

14\. Which day generates the most revenue?

15\. Which month performs best?

16\. Do weekends outperform weekdays?

\---

\#\# Business Recommendations

17\. Which products should management prioritize?

18\. Are staffing adjustments recommended?

19\. Are there inventory opportunities?

20\. What operational improvements are suggested?
